# AURALIS — marketing brief

## Product
**AURALIS** by Field Instruments — a sculptural spatial audio node for design-led homes and focused workspaces.

## Audience
Design-conscious buyers, architects, and portfolio reviewers who judge craft, clarity, and conversion discipline.

## Job to be done
Fill a room with spatial presence without a multi-speaker installation.

## Positioning
Stereo was a compromise. Space is the product.

## Funnel (page architecture)
1. **Awareness** — Hero: brand-first name, one promise, one primary CTA
2. **Shift** — Reframe the category (problem → new belief)
3. **Desire** — System beats (sense → sculpt → stay invisible)
4. **Proof** — Materials + social quotes
5. **Conversion** — Founders Edition invite (single clear CTA)

## Tone
Calm, precise, slightly cinematic. No gadget hype. No AI-purple tech clichés.

## Success metrics (if this shipped)
- Hero → Order scroll depth ≥ 45%
- CTA click-through on Founders Edition ≥ 3.5%
- Time-on-page ≥ 70s (scroll-scrubbed product should drive dwell)
