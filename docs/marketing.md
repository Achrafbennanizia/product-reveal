# AURALIS — product brief

## Product
**AURALIS** — a single-node spatial loudspeaker that measures the room, steers energy with phased beams, and renders height/distance cues using hearing science (ITD/ILD, pinna cues, early reflections).

## Audience
People who want clearer spatial music/film playback without a multi-speaker surround install.

## Job to be done
Place sound in 3D space in a real living room, with measurable calibration — not louder stereo.

## Logic rules (copy must respect these)
1. State the hearing cue before the feature.
2. Prefer measurable claims (degrees, dB, ms, seconds) over adjectives.
3. Never claim “magic”; describe sense → beam → HRTF pipeline.
4. One primary CTA after proof (Founders invite).

## Funnel
1. Hero — what it is in one sentence
2. Problem — why stereo is incomplete
3. Method — three scientific steps
4. Specs — checkable numbers
5. Lab results — metrics
6. Order — clear offer
